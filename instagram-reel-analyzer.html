Q: Is this really free?

Yes! 100% free. No credit card, no sign-up, no hidden costs. We believe every creator deserves access to powerful AI tools.

Q: How long does analysis take?

Most analyses complete in 30-90 seconds. Longer videos may take up to 2 minutes. You'll see real-time progress updates.

Q: What makes this different from other tools?

We use advanced AI trained on millions of viral reels. Our analysis goes beyond basic metrics—we decode the psychology, trends, and specific elements that make content go viral.

Q: Can I analyze private accounts?

No, the reel must be from a public Instagram account. Private account content cannot be accessed or analyzed.

Q: Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. We process URLs securely, analyze the content, and immediately delete all data after analysis. We never store your information or the analyzed videos.
