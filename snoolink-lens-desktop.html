Frequently Asked Questions

Answer the main objections before download.

Does Lens upload my media to the cloud?

Lens is local-first. Media indexing and search can run on your machine without mandatory cloud upload.

How long does indexing take?

It depends on media volume, hardware, and storage speed. First index is the longest; incremental updates are faster.

Can I search video scenes, not just files?

Yes. Lens is designed for scene-level retrieval so you can jump to relevant moments more quickly.

Can teams use the same setup?

Yes. Teams can standardize tagging/query workflows while keeping local-first operational control.
