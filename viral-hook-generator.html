What makes a hook go viral?

  • Pattern interrupt: Start with a line that breaks expectation immediately.
  • Curiosity gap: Promise a payoff while withholding just enough detail.
  • Bold claim: A surprising statement creates instant attention.
  • Relatable pain: Mirror what the audience is frustrated with right now.

Weak vs Strong Hook Examples

Weak: "Here is my morning workout routine."

Strong: "I fixed my energy in 7 days with this routine."

Weak: "These are my travel tips for Europe."

Strong: "Stop overpaying in Europe. Do this first."

Weak: "This is what I eat for protein."

Strong: "Protein meals under 10 minutes that actually taste good."
